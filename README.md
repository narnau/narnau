## What I’m building now

I’m building [**YapScore**](https://yapscore.ai/)
 — an AI-powered music notation editor that lets you edit and generate sheet music using natural language.
Instead of navigating complex notation software, you just describe what you want:
- “Transpose the whole piece to G major”
- “Add a crescendo in measures 4–8”
- “Write a 16-bar jazz waltz in Bb for piano”

YapScore understands the musical intent, modifies structured score data (MusicXML), renders the updated notation instantly, and provides in-browser MIDI playback. You can export to standard formats compatible with MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius, Dorico, and other notation tools.
